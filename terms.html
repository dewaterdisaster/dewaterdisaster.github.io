Terms of use

Purpose and access

Atlas Evidence Services is a personal research, document-management, and recovery tool. This public website supplies information about that tool. It does not grant access to the private system or collection, and it does not offer a public hosted service.

Authorized operation

Cloud connections and private processing are operated with the account owner's authorization. The owner is responsible for permissions to access and process their records and for reviewing transfer settings, especially deletions.

Accuracy and preservation

Extracted text, metadata, and derived measurements can contain errors or omissions. Important findings should be checked against the identified original source. A document's inclusion is not an endorsement of its assertions. Atlas does not provide legal advice or establish the accuracy of third-party records.

Availability and recovery

The tool is under development. Cloud APIs, network connections, and processing can fail or be interrupted. A synchronized mirror does not replace independent backups. Backup restoration must be tested; service availability and recovery times are not guaranteed.

Privacy and changes

See the privacy information for how data is accessed and used. These pages will be updated as the system changes. No ownership of underlying source records is claimed by this website.
