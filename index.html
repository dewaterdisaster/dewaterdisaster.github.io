Atlas Evidence Services

A private, owner-operated tool for organizing and backing up documents.

This website provides information about the app. It does not provide access to the owner's private document collection.

About the app

When authorized by its owner, Atlas uses Google Drive to list and read files and to create, update, and verify copies in a designated folder. The app is for the owner's personal use; this site does not offer public accounts, uploads, or document search.

Privacy and contact

Read the privacy information and terms of use. For questions, email dewaterdisaster@gmail.com. You can also use the project's public GitHub issues. Issues are public: do not include documents, personal information, passwords, or access tokens.
