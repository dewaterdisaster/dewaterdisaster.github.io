Atlas Evidence Services

A private tool for organizing source documents, preserving provenance, and maintaining recoverable copies of a research collection.

This website provides application and privacy information. It does not provide access to the private document collection or server.

What the tool does

Atlas supports owner-authorized copying between OneDrive and Google Drive, encrypted recovery archives, and a PostgreSQL catalog of document identities, locations, and processing history. The collection includes public-records requests, court records, reports, maps, and environmental datasets.

The system is being developed and tested. Recurring mirroring and expanded retrieval features are not yet in production.

Why Google Drive access is requested

The owner authorizes the tool to list and read files, upload backups, verify copies, and manage a designated mirror folder in Google Drive. The intended mirror follows its OneDrive source, including updates and deletions. Recovery archives are kept separately from the mirror.

Private operation

Atlas is operated for the owner's own collection. This site does not offer public account registration, file uploads, document search, or a connection to the private database.

Data practices and contact

Read the privacy information and terms of use. Project questions can be raised through the project's GitHub issues. Issues are public: do not include documents, personal information, passwords, or access tokens.
