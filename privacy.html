Data practices · November 1, 2026

Privacy information

Atlas Evidence Services is a private, owner-operated tool. This page explains how it handles information accessed through Google Drive and information collected by this public website.

Google Drive information

With the owner's authorization, the app may access Google Drive file and folder names, identifiers, contents, and related details such as locations, sizes, dates, and available checksums.

Use and storage

This information is used to organize and verify owner-directed copies and backups. Selected files may be copied to other storage locations chosen by the owner. Files and related records may be held in the owner's cloud storage accounts or on the owner's private server. Google and other storage providers process information stored with them under their own policies.

Sharing and AI

The app does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models. The current Google Drive connection does not provide document access to an external AI service.

Retention and control

Files and copies are retained according to the owner's decisions. Deleting a file from a mirror may remove its destination copy; separately kept backups may retain earlier copies until the owner removes them.

The owner can revoke the app's access through Google Account settings for third-party connections. Revoking access stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete existing copies held in cloud accounts or on the private server.

Security

The owner uses safeguards to protect the collection and its copies. No online service can guarantee against every loss or unauthorized access.

Google API commitments

Atlas Evidence Services' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

This public website

The site has no analytics scripts, advertising, or document-upload form. GitHub Pages hosts it and logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Questions

For non-sensitive questions, email dewaterdisaster@gmail.com. You can also use the project's public GitHub issues. Do not post private documents, credentials, or personal records there. The support email shown on Google's authorization screen is configured separately from this website.
